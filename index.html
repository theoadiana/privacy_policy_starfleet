Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Starfleet Last Orbit, a mobile game developed and published by the developer of Starfleet Last Orbit. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the game.

Starfleet Last Orbit does not require players to create an account to play the game. However, third-party services integrated into the game may collect certain information automatically, including device information, advertising identifiers, usage information, diagnostic data, and information related to advertising and app interactions, as permitted by applicable law.

Starfleet Last Orbit may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, and interactions with advertisements to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent, applicable laws, and available settings.

For more information about how Google processes information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the game may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent for advertising-related data processing and to provide privacy choices. You may be able to review or change your consent choices through the available privacy options, where applicable.

4. In-App Purchases

Starfleet Last Orbit may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. Payment information is handled by Google according to its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

5. Data Retention

Information collected by third-party services is subject to the policies and retention practices of those services. We do not independently maintain a player account database unless additional features are introduced.

6. Children's Privacy

Starfleet Last Orbit is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The game and its advertising configuration are intended to comply with applicable laws and Google Play policies. If the game is made available to children or mixed audiences, additional child-protection and advertising requirements may apply.

The game may use third-party services, including:
